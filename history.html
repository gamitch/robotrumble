Robot Rumble at the Raceway

DECODE · Saturday, August 29, 2026 · World Wide Technology Raceway

The 2026 Rumble ran the DECODE season game with 28 teams — 14 from Illinois and 14 from Missouri — in the cross-border showcase. The state-vs-state finals went Illinois’ way in two straight matches, 258–174 and 296–111.

Winners — Illinois Alliance Ctrl-Y (12971) & Super Scream Bros (14204) Finalists — Missouri Alliance Why (99919) & Force Green Robotics (10265)

Showdown at the Border

Into the Deep · Saturday, August 30, 2025 · World Wide Technology Raceway

The event grew to 24 teams for the first time, with competition from three states. The Missouri vs. Illinois rivalry intensified on the field at WWT Raceway during the Into the Deep season, and the finals went two straight to the blue alliance, 208–188 and 242–155.

Winners Gear Girls (23469), Knights of the Ping Pong Table (19938) & SeaCow Robotics (21418) Finalists Zenith (23307), Fruity Loops (23424) & Force Green Robotics (10265)
