About the Event

The STL Area’s Offseason Proving Ground

Robot Rumble at the Raceway is the final offseason FIRST Tech Challenge event in the St. Louis region before the new season kicks off in September. This welcoming, low-pressure competition gives teams one last chance to run their robot, test new drive teams, and settle the Illinois–Missouri rivalry inside a real racetrack.

Whether it’s a veteran squad dialing in strategy or a rookie team gaining match experience, the Rumble is built for everyone. Come for the competition, stay for the atmosphere — nothing beats the Raceway.

  • Offseason RepsReal match experience before the season begins
  • New Drive TeamsRotate in new drivers in a low-stakes environment
  • Low-Pressure FormatOffseason showcase — no state-qualifying implications
  • The IL/MO RivalryThe only cross-border FTC showcase in the region
