About the Event

The STL Area's Offseason FTC Event

Robot Rumble at the Raceway is the final offseason FTC event in the St. Louis region before the new season kicks off in September. This welcoming, low-pressure competition gives teams one last opportunity to run their robot, test new drive teams, and enjoy a cross-border showcase with teams from Illinois and Missouri.
